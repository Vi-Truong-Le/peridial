Frequently asked questions

Is my data backed up anywhere?
No. Peridial is a local-first, offline app — everything you create (events, notes, tracked patterns) is stored only on your device. There is no cloud sync, so if you delete the App or lose your device without a personal backup (such as an iCloud device backup you manage yourself), that data cannot be recovered by us.
Does Peridial need an internet connection?
No. Peridial works entirely offline and does not require or use a network connection for any feature.
Why does Peridial ask for notification permission?
Only when you choose to "Track" a pattern, so the App can remind you about it. You can allow or deny this in iOS Settings → Notifications → Peridial at any time without affecting any other feature.
Do you collect any analytics or personal data?
No. See our Privacy Policy for full details.
How do I delete my data?
Delete individual entries inside the App, or delete the App itself from your device to remove all locally stored data at once.
