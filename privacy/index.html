Peridial ("the App") is developed and published by Vi Truong Le ("we", "us", "our"). This Privacy Policy explains how the App handles information when you use it.

Short answer

Peridial is a local-first, fully offline app. It does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal data, because the App has no network connection at all. Everything you enter stays on your device.

Information we do not collect

The App does not use the internet to send or receive data. As a direct result, we do not collect, and cannot access:

  • Your name, email address, or any account information (the App has no accounts or sign-in)
  • Location, contacts, photo library, or microphone data (the App never requests these permissions)
  • Photos you take inside the App — these are written only to the App's local store on your device and are never uploaded, synced, or accessible to us
  • Analytics, usage statistics, crash reports, or advertising identifiers
  • Any content you create inside the App (events, logs, notes, photos, tracked patterns)

Information stored on your device

Peridial stores the data you create — events, tracked patterns, notes, photos taken with the in-app shutter, and app preferences — locally on your device using Apple's SwiftData framework. This data:

  • Never leaves your device
  • Is not synced to any server, cloud account, or third party
  • Is not visible to us, to Vi Truong Le, or to any other party
  • Is deleted when you delete the App, or when you delete individual entries inside the App

Permissions the App requests

Peridial requests two system permissions. Neither is requested at launch, and declining either one leaves the rest of the App working normally.

  • Camera — requested the first time you drag an activity onto the shutter and hold it. The camera is active only while you keep holding the shutter. When you release, the frame is cropped to a circle and saved next to that log in the App's local store on your device. Photos are never uploaded, never written to your Photo Library, and never accessible to us. Peridial has no photo import, no filters, and no editing tools. You can revoke this at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Camera → Peridial; only photo capture stops working.
  • Local Notifications — requested only when you choose to "Track" a pattern, and only to remind you, on your own device, about that pattern. The reminder is scheduled and delivered entirely by iOS, locally, and transmits nothing. You can revoke this at any time in iOS Settings → Notifications → Peridial.

The App does not request microphone, location, contacts, photo library, or any other permission.

Third-party services

Peridial does not integrate any advertising SDK, analytics SDK, or crash-reporting SDK, and does not make any network calls. The App's source contains an inactive internal placeholder for a future Firebase Remote Config integration; this placeholder performs no network activity and collects no data in the current version. If a future version of Peridial activates this or any other third-party service, this Privacy Policy will be updated in advance of that release to describe exactly what is collected and why.

Children's privacy

Because Peridial collects no personal data of any kind, it does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the relevant age of digital consent in your region). If you believe a child has provided personal information through the App, please contact us — though by design, no such information can reach us.

Data retention and deletion

All data lives only in local device storage. To delete your data:

  • Delete individual entries/patterns from within the App, or
  • Delete the App from your device, which permanently removes all locally stored data (SwiftData store) along with it.

We hold no copy of your data, so there is nothing further for us to delete on our end.

Your rights

Because we never receive or store any of your personal data on our servers, there is no personal data for us to export, correct, or erase on your behalf — that control already rests entirely with you, on your device. If you have questions about this, contact us using the details below.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy if the App's functionality changes (for example, if a future version adds a network-connected feature). We will post the revised policy at this same URL and update the effective date above.
