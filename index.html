FAQ
Do I need an account?

No. Peridial opens straight to use — there's no sign-up, login or email required.

Is it really free?

Yes. There are no in-app purchases and no locked features in this version.

Where is my data stored?

Only on your device. Peridial has no servers and collects nothing — it runs fully offline.

How does prediction work?

Peridial learns the average interval and typical time of each event you log, then estimates when the next one is likely due. The more you log, the sharper it gets.

What are Discoveries?

Automatically-found correlations between two event types — shown with a confidence percentage, how much more likely than usual it is, and the sample size.
