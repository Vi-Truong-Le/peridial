Photo logs

FAQ
Do I need an account?

No. Orbit opens straight to use — there's no sign-up, login or email required.

How do I log something?

Press an activity on the dial and drag it into the Now circle in the middle, then let go. To log it with a photo, drag it onto the shutter instead, hold over the front or back half, and release to capture.

Why only 12 activities on the dial?

The dial is a fixed ring: past a dozen, the activities get small enough that dragging one becomes fiddly. Keep as many as you like in Event Types and switch them onto the dial whenever your routine changes.

What happens to my photos?

They're saved next to the log on your device and never uploaded. Orbit doesn't write to your Photo Library, doesn't ask for Photo Library access, and has no filters or editing tools — the point is that a logged photo stays truthful.

Is it really free?

Yes. There are no in-app purchases and no locked features in this version.

Where is my data stored?

Only on your device. Orbit has no servers and collects nothing — it runs fully offline.

How does prediction work?

Give a scheduled activity the times it usually happens and Orbit fills its ring as the next one comes due. Spontaneous activities are never predicted.

What are Discoveries?

Automatically-found correlations between two event types — shown with a confidence percentage, how much more likely than usual it is, and the sample size.
